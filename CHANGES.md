# Simple Elo upgrade: what changed

Branch `feature/population-build-plan`, local only and not pushed. This covers the first parts of the build plan in the information paper, Addendum sections 16 and 17.

## Added

1. **"I can't judge".** The answer is recorded and closes the matchup, but moves no rating. It stays out of win counts, comparison counts and replay. A tie still scores as a draw.
2. **Performance and potential tracks.** Each officer has two ratings. Each round belongs to one track and asks that track's question.
3. **Grade bands.** Tactical (LT/CPT), operational (MAJ/LTC) and strategic (COL and above), derived from rank. Pairs never cross a band, and scoring refuses one that does. The "All bands" view shows no ranking across bands.
4. **Unit and quarter.** Every comparison keeps the units held at the time and its quarter. Dashboard filters cut the record by track, band, unit and quarter. The dashboard also shows the unit link graph and each unit's change in cross-unit win rate.
5. **Budgeted pair selection.** This replaces all-pairs rounds. The default is 10 comparisons per officer (7 to 15 in the UI) with 20% cross-unit pairs. No pair repeats. For 30 officers, 7 and 15 per officer give 105 and 225 comparisons, as in the paper. The round reports its realised share and warns about small pools or unlinked units.
6. **Shuffled replay view.** New Replay page: 100 orders by default, shuffled inside each quarter, with the spread per officer.

The interface was also redesigned, taking its visual direction from usavionix.com.

## Also changed

- Ratings are stored unrounded. The paper's bracket and the replay figures need this.
- The backend now reads `DATABASE_URL`. It previously ignored it, so it could not start outside Docker.
- Editing personnel now works. A DuckDB bug made `UPDATE ... RETURNING` fail.
- A matchup can no longer be answered twice.
- `/api/rate` no longer treats an unknown winner as person A.
- `/api/stats` no longer caps the rating count at 1,000.
- The Skip button is gone. It only reloaded the same pair.
- An existing database is upgraded in place on start.

## Demo data

`backend/seed_demo.py` builds a separate database, and every page labels it synthetic. All data is synthetic.

- **Performance track:** the simulation's 108,000 comparisons for 3,000 officers. Replaying them reproduces the simulation's ratings at every quarter with zero drift, and the app's cross-unit cut reproduces its unit figures (U07 first, +18.1 points).
- **Potential track:** generated by the seed script, because the simulation has no potential answers. It uses a third of the pairs and an assumed 0.6 correlation with performance.
- **Ties and can't-judge:** both start at zero, because the simulation models neither.
- **Simulation files:** read from the Fellowship Package folder and not copied into the repo, because the paper lists them as not published.

## Decisions to confirm

- **Promotion across a band** restarts both ratings at 1000 and keeps the old record under the old band. The paper does not address this.
- **Enlisted ranks** stay in the app in a separate "unbanded" pool. Warrant officers have no band yet.

## Not done

- **Pooled observers.** The rater is still blank on every answer, so nothing yet enforces more than one observer per officer. The simulation found this matters.
- Bradley-Terry scoring, evidence view, audit trail and consistency screens.
- The displayed rating is still the sequential one. Replay shows the spread but does not replace the rating.
- A can't-judge answer leaves that officer one comparison short. The budget is not topped up.
- Docker was not tested. `docker-compose.yml` also points the browser at `http://backend:8000`, which a browser cannot reach.

## Read with care

- If this is pushed, paper Table A-5 ("Simple Elo today") will understate the prototype.
- In the tactical cut, U05 also clears zero (+13.3 ± 9.7) and was not planted. With 20 units, expect some false leads.

## Checks

- 872 backend tests pass. Eight deliberate breaks of the scoring rules were each caught.
- `tsc` and the production build pass.
- A real browser run confirmed the filters, a round, can't-judge and tie, and showed no horizontal scroll at 1440 and 390 wide.
